  What this implies

If iAC averages industry benchmarks, ~30% of inbound leads are lost during scoping. A pre-built Ideation Pack (templated brief, fixed-price audit, NDA boilerplate) typically lifts this stage by 8–12 percentage points.

  • Ideation → Develop is the highest-leverage point
  • Develop → Launch usually loses 10–20% to scope creep
  • Post-launch retention >90% becomes the recurring revenue base
