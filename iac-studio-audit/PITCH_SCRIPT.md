# Pitch Script — iAC Studio Demo

You have **two deliverables** and they pitch differently:

| File | What it is | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| `iac_studio_strategy_report.html` | Polished single-file HTML — opens in any browser, no setup | **Send first.** This is the wow factor. |
| `iac_studio_audit.Rmd` | Live, code-driven R Markdown — fetches real data on every render | **Mention as the next step.** Proves you can pull live data once he gives access. |

---

> 2. Audit of your "Wall of Love" — sentiment, verticals, voice of customer
> 3. ASO keyword opportunity gap (where your messaging is losing high-intent traffic)
> 4. Service funnel efficiency — modeled on industry benchmarks
> 5. 2×2 opportunity heatmap + 3 strategic recommendations
> 6. How this becomes a live dashboard once analytics access is shared
>

>

>
> — Ahmad

**Why this works:**
- One sentence per section = he can scan it in 20 seconds
- Ends on the upsell (analytics access) without being pushy
- "Happy to walk through it on a call" gives him a low-friction next step

## 2. The walkthrough call — if he says "let's hop on a call"

Aim for **5–7 minutes** total. Don't read every chart — let him ask.

### Opening (30 seconds)
> "Sir, before I share my screen — quick context. I built this in 48 hours from your public website only. No login, no analytics access, nothing private. So everything you'll see is what Google and a search engine see when they look at iac-studio.com. The point is to show you the *kind* of analysis I do, not to pretend I have the full picture yet."

### Section 1 — Executive Summary (60 seconds)
*Share screen, scroll to top.*
> "Top of the report — two cards. The first one is what your site is doing right: clear focus on Flutter and Unity, founder-led credibility, an active education funnel through Hashlearning. That's a strong story.
>
> The card on the right is what I *cannot* see yet — installs, conversion rates, retention. So I built this gauge — **34% public-data completeness**. That number is the honest ceiling of what any external analyst can do for you. Which is exactly why I want analytics access for round two."

### Section 2 — Wall of Love Audit (60 seconds)
> "Your testimonials are doing real work. I categorised them and the sentiment splits 65% technical praise, 25% student support, 10% delivery speed. That tells me your reputation is *engineering-led*, which is the right reputation if you want to win enterprise contracts.
>
> The bubble chart shows verticals — students dominate, eCommerce is second. Notice fintech and healthtech are present but small. That's a growth lane."

### Section 3 — ASO Keyword Gap (90 seconds — the money slide)
*Scroll to keyword chart.*
> "This is the chart I want you to remember. Blue bars = keywords your homepage actively talks about. Pink bars = high-volume keywords the market is *searching* for that you're silent on.
>
> See this huge pink bar with no blue next to it? That's '**Apple Search Ads agency**'. Same with '**Play Store optimization**' and '**App store keyword ranking**'. These are people with budget, ready to pay an agency, and you're not in the conversation. That's the easiest fix in this entire report — three new landing pages."

### Section 4 — Funnel (45 seconds)
> "I modeled your stated 3-step process — Ideation, Develop, Launch — against agency industry benchmarks. The biggest leakage is always between Ideation and Develop, the scoping phase. A pre-built scoping pack typically lifts that step 8–12 percentage points. We can quantify yours exactly once I see real lead data."

### Section 5 — Heatmap + Recommendations (60 seconds)
*Scroll to 2×2 grid.*
> "Top-left green — your sweet spot. Bottom-right red — skip. The interesting square is bottom-left orange: **Apple Search Ads, TikTok Ads ASO, subscription analytics**. High demand, you don't offer them yet. That's the white space.
>
> And these three recommendations below — dedicated ASO landing pages, two case studies with real numbers, structured data for app schemas. Each one is a low-cost, high-leverage move."

### Section 6 — The close (45 seconds)
> "Last section is the offer. Everything you saw was simulated against industry benchmarks because I had no access. With read-only access to your **Google Search Console**, **Play Console** and **App Store Connect**, this becomes a *live* dashboard — it auto-refreshes every time you open it.
>
> I'd like to do that as a paid engagement. Either a one-time deep audit, or a monthly retainer where I send you this report refreshed with real numbers every 30 days. What works for you?"

## 3. Handling common objections

### "How much do you charge?"
> "Two options. **One-time audit** — fixed fee, you get a report like this but with real numbers, takes 1 week. **Monthly retainer** — I refresh the dashboard every month and we have a 30-min review call. The retainer is cheaper per-report after month 2. I'll send you both prices in writing — just give me a yes on the format and I'll quote within 24 hours."

*(Don't quote a number on the call. Send it in writing so he reads it cold and doesn't haggle live.)*

### "Why should I give you analytics access — that's sensitive."
> "Read-only access. I can't change anything, can't install anything, can't see anything except what Google or Apple already shows you. We can also start with just Search Console — that's the least sensitive one. If round one goes well we add the others."

### "I already know my site has issues, what's new here?"
> "Probably nothing in section 1. The value is sections 3 and 5 — the keyword gap and the white-space services. Those aren't visible from inside your business, only from comparing your site to the rest of the market. That's where I add value over you doing it yourself."

### "I don't really need a data analyst, I need a marketer."
> "Fair. The way I'd frame it — a marketer tells you *what* to do. I tell you *which* of those things will move the needle. Your time is the bottleneck, not the ideas. I cut your shortlist of fixes from 50 to the 3 that matter."

### "Can you also do the implementation?"
> "I focus on the analysis. For implementation I'd partner with your existing dev team — I'll hand them a prioritized spec sheet so they know exactly what to ship. That keeps me independent and keeps the report honest."

>

**Why this works:**
- Adds a tiny piece of new value (so it's not just "any update?")
- Leaves him a graceful exit ("not the right time") which paradoxically makes him more likely to say yes now

## 5. The mental frame — read this before every call

You are **not** asking him for a job. You are offering him a *clearer view of his own business* than he currently has. The audit is the gift; the engagement is the natural next step. If he doesn't see the value in 5 minutes, that's data — move on, the next founder will.

You analyzed his entire public footprint in 48 hours with no access. That alone proves you are not the average freelancer. Lead with that confidence, not nerves.
